# Pitch document

Use when an implemented or well-defined proposal needs a short decision from a reviewer or stakeholder.

1. Open with a concrete demonstration or before/after illustration when one exists. Label simulations.
2. State the proposal in one sentence and support the problem statement with a dated source. If the evidence is incomplete, say so plainly.
3. Answer the most likely objections in collapsed sections, linking each answer to a decision, test, or observation.
4. Show the proposed choice, a realistic alternative, and the tradeoff. Name who must actually approve if the host workflow defines approvers.
5. End with reply options: proceed, revise a specific point, or stop.

The artifact communicates a decision. It does not record a sign-off until the authorized person explicitly gives one through the host workflow.
